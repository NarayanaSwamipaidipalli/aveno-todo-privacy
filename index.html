1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how the Aveno To-Do Android application (the “app”) handles information. It covers the app itself and the software components from Google that the app uses to show advertisements.

Aveno To-Do is an offline-first task manager. It works without an internet connection, does not ask you to create an account, and does not upload your tasks anywhere. The only part of the app that uses the internet is optional banner advertising provided by Google AdMob, described in sections 5 to 7.

In this policy, “Aveno”, “we” and “us” mean the developer of the app. By installing or using the app you acknowledge the practices described here.

4. Offline task-data storage

Everything you enter in Aveno To-Do is stored locally on your device, in storage that Android reserves for the app. Creating, editing, completing, searching and deleting tasks all work without an internet connection, and none of these actions sends data over the network.

Backup and restore

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your tasks are not copied to your Google account by the system.

You can create a backup yourself from Settings › Your data › Save a backup. This writes your tasks, subtasks, lists and completion history to a JSON file in a location you pick with Android's file picker. Please note:

  • The backup file is readable text and is not encrypted. Anyone who can open the file can read your tasks.
  • If you save the file to a cloud storage app (for example a drive or file-sharing service), that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Backup files are not deleted when you delete tasks or uninstall the app. You need to delete them yourself.

Restoring a backup replaces the tasks and lists currently in the app, and only happens after you confirm it.

5. Google AdMob advertising

Aveno To-Do is free and is supported by banner advertisements delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show ads, the app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Ads are banners only, and appear only on the Home and Lists screens. The calendar, statistics, settings, task editor and notifications contain no ads.
  • Ads are requested only when your device is online. Offline, the app shows no ads and makes no advertising requests.
  • You can switch ads off at any time under Settings › Privacy & advertising › Allow banner ads. Every task feature keeps working with ads off.
  • Your task content is never sent to Google or to any advertiser. Ads are not selected based on what you write in the app.

Consent

Where privacy law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If you do not consent to personalised advertising, Google may show non-personalised ads, which still use some device information for purposes such as frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. If your consent status cannot be confirmed, the app does not request ads for that session.

Where a consent choice applies to you, an Advertising privacy choices button appears in Settings so you can review or change your decision at any time.

Google explains how it uses information from apps that use its advertising services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Advertising identifiers and device information

When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and send to Google the following information, as described in Google's data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK:

  • Advertising and device identifiers – the Android advertising ID (a resettable identifier provided by Google Play services), the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.
  • IP address – which may be used to estimate your general location (for example, country or city). The app never requests access to your device's precise location.
  • Ad interactions – such as when the app is opened, and which ads are shown or tapped.
  • Diagnostic information – performance information about the app and the advertising software, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.

Like any internet request, ad requests also carry general technical details such as the device model, operating system version and language setting.

Google uses this information for advertising (including personalised ads where you have allowed them), analytics and measurement, and fraud prevention. Aveno does not receive or store these identifiers.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads). Deleting it prevents apps from using it to show you personalised ads. You can also manage ad personalisation for your Google account in Google My Ad Center.

9. Notifications and reminders

Reminders are created and delivered entirely on your device. The app does not use a push-notification server, so the content of your reminders is never sent over the internet.

  • A reminder notification displays the title of the task. On a locked screen, Android hides or shows this according to your lock-screen notification settings.
  • You can turn reminders off in the app under Settings › Reminders, or withdraw the notification permission in Android's settings at any time.
  • Reminders are scheduled through Android's background system and may be delayed by battery-saving features.

The app does not send marketing or promotional notifications.

10. Data retention

  • Task data and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, replace them by restoring a backup, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Aveno keeps no copy.
  • Backup files you export remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Advertising data collected by Google is retained by Google according to its own policies. See how Google retains data.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request, and then deleted.

11. Data deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time, without contacting us:

  • Delete individual tasks or lists inside the app. Deleting a task also cancels its reminder.
  • Delete everything by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings › Apps › Aveno To-Do › Storage › Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. Both remove all tasks, lists and preferences from the device.
  • Delete backup files you exported, from the location where you saved them.

There is no account to delete, because the app does not create one. Aveno holds no copy of your tasks and therefore cannot recover them after you delete them.

To limit or delete advertising data held by Google, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings and use Google My Ad Center or the controls described in the Google Privacy Policy.

12. Data sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your tasks, notes, lists or reminders with anyone. They never leave your device unless you export a backup yourself.
  • The advertising and device data described in section 6 is collected directly by Google through its software in the app, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This is the only data that is shared with a third party.
  • We may disclose information you have emailed to us if we are legally required to do so. We have no other information about you to disclose.

13. Security

Your tasks are kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. On current Android devices this storage is also protected by the device's own encryption when a screen lock is set.

The app does not add a separate layer of encryption to its database, and exported backup files are not encrypted. Anyone with unlocked access to your device, or to a backup file, may be able to read your tasks. We recommend using a screen lock and storing backup files only in places you trust.

According to Google's documentation, data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Children's privacy

Aveno To-Do is a general-audience productivity app. It is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required for consent to data processing in your country, and it is not part of a children's or families programme.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for a name, age, email address or any other personal detail. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

15. Privacy rights and choices

Choices available in the app and on your device

  • Turn ads off under Settings › Privacy & advertising › Allow banner ads.
  • Change your advertising consent with the Advertising privacy choices button in Settings, shown where a consent choice applies to you.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in your device's settings.
  • Turn reminders off in the app or in Android's notification settings.
  • Use the app fully offline. No task feature requires the internet.
  • Take a copy of your data by exporting a backup, and delete your data as described in section 11.

Your legal rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act may give you rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because Aveno does not hold your tasks or any identifier for you, most of these rights can be exercised directly on your device using the controls above. For data collected by Google through advertising, you can use the tools in the Google Privacy Policy and My Ad Center. If you need help with any request, email us at roughnarayana@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.
